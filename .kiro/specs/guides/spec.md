# Spec: Clinical Guides Tab

## Requirements
1. **WHEN** the Guides tab is opened, **THE SYSTEM SHALL** display educational guide cards for each maternal need in App Voice.
2. **WHEN** medical advice or clinical amounts are discussed, **THE SYSTEM SHALL** conclude with "Your doctor decides the amount".
3. **WHEN** tests, visits, and vaccines are mentioned, **THE SYSTEM SHALL** frame them strictly as questions for the mother to ask her physician.
