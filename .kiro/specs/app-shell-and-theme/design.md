# Design: App Shell and Theme

## Theme Tokens & Colors
- **Storybook Night Nursery Palettes:**
  - `Everyday`:
    - Sky Gradient: `#3B2A4F` to `#2E2040`
    - Paper (cards): `#FBF3E6`
    - Ink: `#2E2040`
    - Accent: `#9A3F6B`
    - Moon: `#F5E2B0`
  - `Halloween`:
    - Sky Gradient: `#1A1224` to `#120C19`
    - Paper (cards): `#2A1F38`
    - Ink: `#F3E9DA`
    - Accent: `#F0A45C`
    - Moon (Pumpkin): `#F0A45C`
- All text-to-background combinations meet or exceed 4.5:1 contrast.

## Typography
- Titles & Baby voice: `Fraunces` (OFL) or serif fallback.
- Body, navigation & App voice: `Atkinson Hyperlegible` / system sans-serif.

## Navigation Shell
- Bottom navigation bar with 3 tabs: Today, This Week, Guides.
- Top bar with mode lamp switch, gestational week chip, and Pause action.
